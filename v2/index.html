The three sessions
Session rule: Weeks 1–2 = 2 rounds. Most later weeks = 3. Rest 45–90 sec when needed. Stop each set with roughly 1–2 clean reps still available.
12-week progression
WEEK 01

Learn it

2 rounds. Find safe ranges and the right push-up level.

WEEK 02

Repeat cleanly

2 rounds. Add 1–2 reps only where easy.

WEEK 03

Add volume

Move to 3 rounds; stay mid-range.

WEEK 04

Own it

3 rounds. Make the third look like the first.

WEEK 05

Slow tempo

3-sec lowering on push-ups, squats and split squats.

WEEK 06

Control

Keep tempo; build plank times gradually.

WEEK 07

Progress one

Harder version of one exercise only.

WEEK 08

Deload

Back to 2 rounds. Keep pattern, dump fatigue.

WEEK 09

Rebuild

3 rounds with the versions you earned.

WEEK 10

Build shape

Work toward top of clean rep ranges.

WEEK 11

Strongest week

Best controlled sets. Still no failure chasing.

WEEK 12

Benchmark

Record clean reps/holds; final Sunday photos.
